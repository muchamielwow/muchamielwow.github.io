WOW FOREVER · CLASS GUIDE

Paladin

Updated October 31, 2026

OverviewLeveling 1-60How to playLevel 60PvPProfessionsStill to comeMacrosCalculator

Overview

The paladin is a plate-wearing holy warrior who hits, endures and heals himself. In Forever it plays far smoother than in Classic: a new strike from level 6 (Holy Strike), Judgement no longer eats your seal, Consecration and Blessing of Kings come from the trainer, and every tree has new talents. Pick it if you want a sturdy, hard-to-kill class that can do it all: damage, tank or healer.

Strengths

  • Very hard to kill: plate, self-heals, Divine Protection from level 6 and Divine Shield from 34.
  • Simple rotation: keep a seal up, press Judgement and Holy Strike whenever they are ready.
  • Wanted in every group for its blessings (Might, Wisdom, Kings) and Salvation.
  • All three trees work at 60: melee damage, Seal of Fury tank or healer.
  • Lay on Hands drops to a 20-minute cooldown (1 hour in Classic): a full heal in the bank for most hard fights.

Weaknesses

  • Low mobility: no charges or leaps. Only Blessing of Freedom and the Pursuit of Justice talent.
  • Mana runs dry if you chain fights or spam Consecration.
  • Many abilities scale with spell power, which is hard to find on plate gear.
  • On the Horde side it can only be Undead.
  • One tree per role: if you want to deal damage, Retribution is your only option. If a patch leaves it weak, there's no other damage tree to switch to.

The specs

RetributionMelee DPS · Leveling, solo play and PvP
Slow two-hander, Seal of Command and holy strikes. The new talents (Sanctified Judgement, Sacred Arbiter, Twist of Light) save mana and boost Holy Strike and seal damage.
ProtectionTank · Tanking dungeons and raids
Shield, Seal of Fury and Consecration. Its Judgement taunts and blocks give mana back. Templar's Bulwark (a shield worth 100% of your health) and Holy Shield are its big defensives.
HolyHealer · Healing groups and raids
Holy Light and Flash of Light with great mana efficiency. Holy Shock drops to a 10 s cooldown and Light's Vigil finally gives a group heal. Its strength is still heavy healing on a single target, usually the tank. It also allows a hybrid Holy Shock damage build.

Recommended races

Human (Alliance)Ruthless gives +1% crit with everything, The Human Spirit +10% Spirit (more mana between fights) and Will to Survive breaks stuns in PvP. The most complete pick.
Dwarf (Alliance)Artisan's Precision gives +1% hit and Stoneform removes bleeds, poisons and diseases and cuts physical damage by 10% for 8 s. Great for tanks and for PvP against rogues.
Undead (Horde)The only Horde paladin race. Cannibalize restores health and mana from corpses (saves food and water while leveling), Will of the Forsaken breaks fears and Touch of the Grave sometimes drains health on hit.

Leveling 1-60

Leveling spec: Retribution

Level as Retribution. Every guide agrees, and it makes sense: Benediction makes every instant cheaper, Seal of Command on a slow two-hander hits hard from level 20, and Sanctified Judgement refunds mana on every Judgement. If you want to tank or heal at max level, still level as Ret and respec at 60. The build below ends on 8/8/35, the most used Retribution setup at 60.

How to kill while leveling

  1. Before you start: Blessing of Might (or Wisdom if mana is tight) and Retribution Aura.
  2. Put your seal up before the pull: Seal of Righteousness until 20, then Seal of Command with a slow two-hander.
  3. Judgement on arrival and whenever it is ready. It no longer eats the seal, so no need to recast it.
  4. Holy Strike every time it is ready (10 s).
  5. Dangerous enemy: Hammer of Justice, then Judgement with Seal of Command, which deals double damage to a stunned or incapacitated target.
  6. Two or more enemies: Consecration (from 20). Against one, do not spend mana on it.
  7. Exorcism on Undead and Demons; Hammer of Wrath below 20% (from 44).
  8. From 40, with Twist of Light: swap from Seal of Command to Seal of Righteousness just before a swing; that swing applies both seals. Optional: more damage, more button presses.

Which seal to use

  • Seal of Righteousness: Holy damage on every swing. Levels 1-20, and always with one-handers or fast weapons.
  • Seal of Command (talent, 20): sometimes adds a Holy hit worth 70% of weapon damage. The slower the weapon, the better.
  • Seal of Fury (10): for tanking. Better with fast weapons and needs a shield for the absorb.
  • Seal of Wisdom (38) and Seal of Light (30): mana or health on hit. Useful in groups as a Judgement on the boss.
  • Seal of Justice (22): sometimes stuns, and its Judgement stops fleeing. For PvP and runners.
  • Seal of the Crusader (6): only to apply its Judgement (more Holy damage taken) and switch back.

Tips

  • Look for slow two-handers: Holy Strike and Seal of Command scale with weapon damage. A good weapon matters more than any other piece.
  • Do the level 20 class quest: it can reward a very strong weapon for that range.
  • Spend mana early in the fight (seal, Judgement, Holy Strike) and let your weapon finish the mob while you regen.
  • If a fight goes wrong: Divine Protection or Divine Shield and two Holy Lights. Lay on Hands is the last resort.
  • Do not swap Stamina and Spirit gear for Strength gear just because it is plate.
  • With the Legacy perk Talented you get your first point at level 5: follow the same order five levels earlier.
  • Undead: use Cannibalize on humanoid or undead corpses and save food and water.
  • In groups, turn off Righteous Fury if you are not tanking and give Salvation to everyone except the tank.
  • Weapons you can learn: one- and two-handed axes, maces and swords, plus polearms. No daggers, staves, fist weapons or ranged weapons. Your race already knows some; the rest come from a weapon master. Each skill costs 10 silver from a capital-city weapon master and can be learned from level 1; polearms cost 1 gold and need level 20. Since Retribution lives on a slow two-hander, learn two-handed swords and axes early so you never pass up a good one, and polearms at 20.

How to play

In PvP

Stun, then unload

Stun + burst (Hammer of Justice into Judgement)

With Seal of Command up, get close and press Hammer of Justice (10 yards, stuns up to 6 sec depending on rank). Right after, Judgement: against a stunned or incapacitated target it deals double damage. Follow with Holy Strike and your swings while the stun lasts. Judgement and Hammer of Justice only reach 10 yards: if you get kited, they won't land. In Forever Judgement doesn't use up the seal, so you can judge again 10 sec later without reapplying it.

Control them so you can heal

Crowd control for a heal / Repentance

Your biggest heal, Holy Light, is slow and easy to interrupt. Cast it while the enemy is controlled: after Hammer of Justice, or after Repentance (Retribution talent, 20 yards, incapacitates humanoids for up to 6 sec). Any damage wakes a Repentance target, so don't hit or judge them until you're done healing. Remember that when you do go back in, Seal of Command's Judgement also doubles against incapacitated targets.

Blessing of Freedom when they slow you

Anti-kite / Blessing of Freedom

Paladins lack mobility, and mages, hunters and warriors thrive on slowing you. Blessing of Freedom makes you immune to roots and slows for 10 sec (20 sec cooldown). Don't use it early: wait until they land Hamstring, Frost Nova, Crippling Poison or Concussive Shot, then use it to close in and stun. With Pursuit of Justice (Retribution) you're always 15% faster.

Fake the heal to bait the interrupt

Juke / fake cast

An interrupt (Kick, Pummel, Shield Bash, Counterspell) locks the whole Holy school, and nearly everything you have is Holy: get interrupted and you have no heals for several seconds. When a rogue or warrior is on you, start Holy Light and cancel it by stepping halfway through the cast. If they burn the interrupt on the fake, you can heal safely. Forever has spell batching: if you both press at almost the same moment, both effects can go through.

Heal behind a pillar

Line of sight (LoS) / pillar humping

Against casters and hunters, heal out of their sight. Put a pillar, a house or a rock between you and them: if they can't see you they can't cast on you, and they have to walk into your 10 yard range. Also use it to let your mana come back or to wait out their effects.

Choose which defensive to spend

Forbearance / defensive cooldowns

Divine Protection, Divine Shield, Blessing of Protection and Templar's Bulwark apply Forbearance for 1 minute, which locks out all the others. Divine Shield (12 sec) is the best one: in Forever you can keep attacking, but at half damage, so use it to heal, Cleanse yourself or wait for help. Blessing of Protection only stops physical attacks: great against a rogue or warrior, useless against a mage.

Don't stun a target that was just stunned

Diminishing returns (DR)

Against players, stuns repeated in a short time last half as long, then a quarter, until the target is immune for a while. If a rogue on your side just used Kidney Shot, your Hammer of Justice may barely last: save it for when the target breaks away or to stop a heal. Repentance is a different family (incapacitate) and isn't shortened by stuns.

In dungeons and raids

Two seals on one swing

Seal twisting (Twist of Light)

From level 40, the Twist of Light talent makes Classic-style seal twisting simpler: when you swap Seal of Command for Seal of Righteousness you keep an Echo of the old seal, and your next swing applies both. The comfortable way is to swap right after a weapon swing lands, so you have the whole gap of a slow weapon to do it. On the next swing go back to Seal of Command and repeat. It costs mana (though the talent cuts seal costs by 20%): do it on bosses or long fights, not on everything.

Spend your mana wisely

Mana management / five-second rule

Your mana only regenerates from Spirit once 5 sec have passed since you last spent any (the five-second rule). While leveling, spend early in the fight (seal, Judgement, Holy Strike) and let your weapon finish the job: if the next swing will kill, don't press anything. Don't refresh your seal before it runs out, only use Consecration on two or more enemies and, if you keep running dry, swap Might for Blessing of Wisdom.

Threat as Protection

Threat (tanking)

To tank, keep Righteous Fury on and Seal of Fury up. Open with Judgement: with Seal of Fury it forces the enemy to attack you for 4 sec, and doubles as a second taunt. Drop Consecration under the pack to hold all of them and use Holy Strike on cooldown. If you're doing damage and not tanking, turn Righteous Fury off.

Start the heal before the hit

Pre-casting / cancel casting

As Holy, Holy Light has a long cast: start it before the boss's big hit, not once the tank is already low. If it turns out you don't need it (the hit misses or the tank is topped), cancel it by stepping before it finishes and you spend no mana. Use Flash of Light for light damage and Holy Shock to save someone right now.

Where to stand

Positioning

As Retribution, always hit from behind: from the front the enemy can parry and block, and you eat its cleaves. Solo leveling, place yourself so Consecration (8 sec to deal its full damage) sits under every enemy, and skip it if you're about to move or they'll die sooner.

Keybinds and macros

  • Turn with the right mouse button and set A/D to strafe: it keeps you on the target's back and lets you cancel a heal with one step without turning.
  • Put Hammer of Justice and Judgement on neighbouring keys (say Q and E): the combo has to come out back to back without looking.
  • Self-heal macro that keeps your target: /cast [@mouseover,help][@player] Flash of Light. Do the same for Blessing of Freedom and Cleanse to use them on an ally by hovering.
  • Put Seal of Command and Seal of Righteousness on two comfortable side-by-side keys: when seal twisting you'll swap seals every swing.

Every technique, explained for any class: Gameplay techniques

Level 60

At 60 there are three clear setups, taken from the most planned builds in the calculator using beta client data. Raids arrive on December 9 and there are no gear lists or real logs yet: the priorities below come from beta-era guides and may change.

Retribution (8/8/35) · Melee DPS

Stats
  • Hit first: a miss deals no damage and applies no seal.
  • Strength and attack power; then crit (feeds Vengeance).
  • Spell power and Intellect: seals, Judgement and Consecration scale with it, and Champion of the Light converts it.
  • A slow two-hander with high damage per hit above everything.
Rotation
  • Seal of Command up; Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown.
  • With Twist of Light: swap to Seal of Righteousness before a swing to apply both seals, then swap back.
  • Consecration on 2+ enemies when mana allows.
  • Hammer of Wrath below 20%; Exorcism on Undead and Demons.
  • In groups, keep up the Judgement the group needs (Wisdom or Light) if no other paladin covers it.
Consumables
  • Well Fed food with Strength or attack power.
  • A Strength or Agility elixir plus a spell power one (if the Classic recipes remain in Forever).
  • Mana potions for long fights.

Protection (0/42/7) · Tank

Stats
  • Survival: Defense, Stamina, armor, block chance and block value.
  • Threat: hit and spell power (most of your damage is Holy), then Strength.
  • Intellect so you do not run dry on long bosses.
  • Fast one-hander (more Seal of Fury hits) and always a shield.
Rotation
  • Righteous Fury, aura and blessing up; Seal of Fury always active.
  • Judgement on the pull (4 s taunt); Swift Judgement for an instant second Judgement early on or as a backup taunt.
  • Holy Shield always up and Consecration under the pack.
  • Holy Strike on cooldown: with Iron Creed it adds threat and cuts damage taken by 10%.
  • Templar's Bulwark for damage spikes (applies Forbearance: do not mix it with Divine Shield).
Consumables
  • Defense and health elixirs (Superior Defense, Fortitude) and Greater Stoneshield Potion for spikes (Classic recipes, unconfirmed in Forever).
  • Mana potions, and some spell power once survival is covered.

Holy (36/10/5) · Healer

Stats
  • Healing power (spell power).
  • Intellect: more mana and more crit.
  • Crit: more healing and more mana back from Illumination.
  • Spirit and mana per 5: Reverence keeps part of your regen going while casting.
Rotation
  • Seal of Wisdom and its Judgement on the boss for mana.
  • Holy Light for big hits (start casting early) or with Infusion of Light up.
  • Flash of Light for light damage; Holy Shock if someone may die now or while moving.
  • Light's Vigil is your only group heal and it is expensive: it marks an ally for 30 sec, and your next Holy Shock on them doesn't trigger its cooldown and heals their whole party. Only one can be active per party: save it for when the whole party takes damage.
  • Divine Favor for a guaranteed crit in an emergency.
Consumables
  • Mana potions and some mana regen (for example Mageblood Potion, if it remains in Forever).
  • Food with Spirit or mana per 5.

Gear

There are no Forever gear lists yet: dungeon loot has been reworked and raids (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia) arrive on December 9. Meanwhile: Blacksmithing makes mail and plate with spell power, close to mandatory early on; Retribution hunts the best slow two-hander; Protection wants a shield and a fast weapon; Holy wants healing power even on cloth or leather. The new 55-60 dungeons (Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace) are a good place to start.

Plan your own in the calculator

PvP at level 60

  • Your key Retribution combo: Hammer of Justice, Judgement with Seal of Command (double damage on a stunned target), then Holy Strike.
  • Repentance also counts as incapacitated for Seal of Command, but any damage breaks it: use it to heal or to set up the burst.
  • Blessing of Freedom against warriors, hunters and Frost mages: without it you never reach melee.
  • Divine Shield no longer slows your swings, but it halves your damage: use it to heal up or drop effects, not to attack.
  • From 42, Cleanse removes magic: clear roots, fears and debuffs from teammates in battlegrounds.
  • Divine Protection, Blessing of Protection, Divine Shield and Templar's Bulwark share Forbearance: track which one you used.
  • Against casters, Voice of Truth (Holy talent) gives 6 s of immunity to silences and interrupts to land a safe heal.
  • Engineering is still very strong in PvP: an extra stun from grenades swings duels.
