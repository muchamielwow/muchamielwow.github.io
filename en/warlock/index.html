WOW FOREVER · CLASS GUIDE

Warlock

Updated October 30, 2026

OverviewLeveling 1-60How to playLevel 60PvPProfessionsStill to comeMacrosCalculator

Overview

The Warlock is a caster who fights with a demon at their side and spends their own health as a resource. Forever reworks it a lot: Curse of Agony and Curse of Doom are now «Banes» (you can keep one Bane and one Curse on the same target), demons scale with your gear, and every tree has a new capstone (Wrack, Demonic Pact and Incinerate). Pick it if you like leveling solo, rarely stopping to rest, and bringing summons and stones to your group.

Strengths

  • One of the best solo levelers: your demon takes the hits while your damage over time does the killing.
  • Almost never stops to drink: Life Tap turns health into mana and Drain Life gives the health back.
  • Plenty of crowd control: Fear, Howl of Terror, Death Coil, Banish and the Succubus or Incubus Seduction.
  • Utility everyone wants: Healthstones, Soulstones and Ritual of Summoning (in Forever the group finder does not teleport you).
  • Demons scale with your stats, so your pet stays useful all the way to 60.
  • A free mount at level 40 from a class quest, which saves you the mount gold.

Weaknesses

  • Wears cloth: if your demon dies or several enemies reach you, you go down fast.
  • Relies on Soul Shards for almost everything: demons, stones and summons.
  • Many channeled spells: interrupts or moving out of range cut them short (drains break as soon as you leave range).
  • Fear has a 20-yard range in Forever, and a feared enemy can come back with friends if you are careless.
  • You juggle a lot at once: demon, damage over time effects, health, mana and shards.

The specs

AfflictionRanged DPS (damage over time) · Solo leveling and raids
You stack Corruption, Bane of Agony and Siphon Life and let them work while you drain. In Forever damage over time can crit (Malevolence and Pandemic build on that), and the capstone, Wrack, is a channel that boosts your other Shadow damage over time. It is the most recommended leveling tree.
DemonologyPet DPS with high survivability · Safe play, PvP and anyone who wants a strong demon
Empowers your demon (Unholy Power, Demonic Knowledge, Demonic Brand) and you (Demonic Embrace, Soul Link). With the capstone, Demonic Pact, you can sacrifice one demon and summon another without losing the buff. It comes online later than Affliction, but it is very solid once the key talents are in.
DestructionRanged DPS (direct damage) · Dungeons, burst damage and PvP
Fire and Shadow up front: Immolate, Conflagrate (now from level 25), Shadowburn and the new Incinerate as its capstone. Shadow and Flame stops Conflagrate from eating Immolate and refunds Shadowburn's shard. It is the hardest tree to level solo, but it shines in groups.

Recommended races

Human (Alliance)Ruthless gives 1% crit and The Human Spirit 10% more Spirit, which in Forever raises what Life Tap returns. Will to Survive breaks stuns in PvP.
Gnome (Alliance)Expansive Mind gives 5% more mana and Escape Artist frees you from slows. Eureka! only affects non-periodic damage, so it suits Destruction better than Affliction.
Orc (Horde)Blood Fury raises spell power by 10% and Command (back in Blizzard's last racial pass) gives your demon 5% more damage. Hardiness shortens stuns. Great for Demonology.
Undead (Horde)Will of the Forsaken breaks Fear, Charm and Sleep, which is gold in PvP. Touch of the Grave heals you as you deal damage and Cannibalize restores health and mana.
Troll (Horde)New as a Warlock in Forever. Berserking speeds up casts (not periodic damage) and Rapid Regeneration heals half your health, which you can then Life Tap into mana.

Leveling 1-60

Leveling spec: Affliction

Affliction is the safest pick and the one nearly every guide recommends. Your demon (the Voidwalker from level 10) takes the hits, you apply damage over time and finish with a wand until 30. From there you switch to «drain tanking»: Drain Life heals you while it deals damage and you barely stop between fights. Life Tap when you have more health than mana, Drain Life when you have more mana than health. If you would rather have a very strong demon and lean less on gear, Demonology also levels very well from around 25-30.

How to kill while leveling

  1. Send your demon in first and let the enemy lock onto it.
  2. Bane of Agony (instant, good for tagging) and Corruption.
  3. Immolate and Siphon Life only on enemies that will live long enough to take the full damage.
  4. Up to 30: finish with your wand. From 30: Drain Life until it dies; switch to the wand if you are at full health or the enemy is nearly dead.
  5. If Nightfall makes Shadow Bolt instant, cast it right away.
  6. From 40: Wrack when the target will last, to boost all your damage over time.
  7. Life Tap whenever your health is higher than your mana. Finish with Drain Soul when you need shards.
  8. Two enemies: put your effects on one and Fear it; fight the other with your demon and Fear the first again when it comes back.

Demons, Soul Shards, stones and summoning

  • Imp (level 1, learned at the start): ranged fire damage, Blood Pact (more Stamina for the group), Fire Shield and Phase Shift. Weak for leveling after 10, but useful in groups for Blood Pact. Costs no shard.
  • Voidwalker (level 10, class quest): the tank. Torment and Suffering pull enemies onto it, Consume Shadows heals it out of combat and Sacrifice gives you an emergency shield. Starting quests: Gakin's Summons (Human, Goldshire), The Slaughtered Lamb (Gnome, Ironforge), Gan'rul's Summons (Orc and Troll, Razor Hill), Halgar's Summons (Undead, Brill).
  • Succubus / Incubus (level 20, a class quest for each): the highest-damage demon. Lash of Pain, Soothing Kiss, Lesser Invisibility and Seduction (controls a humanoid for up to 15 s; damage breaks it). The Incubus is new in Forever and shares the Succubus spells. The Horde Succubus chain is long: get the Barrens and Stonetalon flight paths first.
  • Felhunter (level 30, class quest): Devour Magic (removes a harmful magic effect from an ally or a helpful one from an enemy), Tainted Blood, Paranoia and Spell Lock from 36 (silence and interrupt). The best for PvP and against casters.
  • Infernal (level 50, Inferno): lands like a meteor and stuns enemies in the area for 2 s. It obeys you for 5 minutes; after that you must Subjugate Demon it or it turns on you. Needs an Infernal Stone, has a 1-hour cooldown and only works outdoors.
  • Doomguard (level 60): through Ritual of Doom (you plus 4 party members and a Demonic Figurine; it sacrifices a random participant and must be subjugated at once) or, with luck, when an enemy dies to Bane of Doom.
  • Subjugate Demon (level 30): takes control of a wild demon (rank 1 works up to level 45 demons) for up to 5 minutes. Costs a shard.
  • Demons in Forever: they scale with your stats, Aggressive mode is back (pet tab of the spellbook) and your current demon is stunned while you summon a new one. Bind Attack and Follow to easy keys.
  • Soul Shards: Drain Soul gives them (in Forever, a chance on each tick and a sure one if the enemy dies while drained; it must be an enemy that gives experience). Shadowburn also does if the enemy dies within 8 s. They are spent on: Voidwalker, Succubus, Incubus, Felhunter, Healthstone, Soulstone, Firestone, Spellstone, Ritual of Summoning, Subjugate Demon, Shadowburn and Soul Fire. The soul bag goes in the reagent bag slot.
  • Stones: Healthstone (level 10) heals instantly; Soulstone (level 18) lets whoever holds it resurrect; Firestone (28) and Spellstone (36) are now temporary weapon enchants: Firestone gives 1-2% spell crit and more Fire damage; Spellstone, 1-2% spell haste and more Shadow damage. Conjured items vanish if you stay logged out for more than 15 minutes.
  • Summoning: Ritual of Summoning (level 20) brings a party member to you. You need two others to click the portal, stay still and be out of combat. In the beta it cost 1 shard; the pre-launch change makes it 5 shards, all of your base mana and a 1-hour cooldown (to be confirmed at launch). Since the Forever group finder does not teleport you, keep spare shards and don't waste it on trips people can walk.

Tips

  • A wand from level 1 (about 15 minutes): sell what you start with until you have about 2 silver, learn Enchanting from your city trainer and buy a Copper Rod, two Mote of Magic and a Simple Wood. Craft the Runed Copper Rod (Copper Rod + Mote of Magic) and then the Novice's Practice Wand (Simple Wood + Mote of Magic), usable from level 1. Finishing with a wand saves mana from the very first minute. If you don't want Enchanting later, you can unlearn it.
  • Get a wand as early as possible: it saves mana until 30. Enchanting makes the first ones, and Gravestone Scepter (Blackfathom Deeps) is one of the best before 30.
  • Keep Demon Skin or Demon Armor up at all times.
  • Health is a resource: Life Tap, then eat, or use First Aid bandages while an enemy runs from Fear.
  • Do not put damage over time on an enemy that is about to die: it only pays off if it has time to tick.
  • One Bane and one Curse can sit on the same target: Bane of Agony for damage and whatever Curse the fight calls for.
  • Use a focus target on a modifier key to DoT or Fear the second enemy without switching targets.
  • Do your demon quests as soon as you hit the level: the Voidwalker at 10 changes leveling completely.
  • If you have the «Talented» Legacy perk, your first talent comes at level 5: start with Improved Corruption all the same.
  • Pull ranged enemies around a corner so they walk to you and away from their pack.
  • Weapons you can learn: daggers, one-handed swords and staves; wands are built in and need no training. Your race already knows one; the rest come from a weapon master. Each skill costs 10 silver from a capital-city weapon master and can be learned from level 1; polearms cost 1 gold and need level 20. Learn all three early so you can equip any Intellect, Stamina or spell power weapon that drops: the weapon gives stats and the wand finishes enemies while your damage over time does the rest.

Level faster with the 5 experience boosts (food, sleeping bag, tent, inn and Legacy).

How to play

In PvP

DoT and run

DoT kiting (DoT and run)

The warlock's take on strafing away (some streams call it «the L»): apply your instant damage-over-time effects and back off while they tick. As Affliction: Bane of Agony, Corruption (instant with Improved Corruption 5/5; 2 sec cast without it), Siphon Life and Curse of Exhaustion (30% slow; more with Amplify Curse first). Move away sideways with the strafe keys, turning the camera with the mouse to keep them in front, and refresh whatever expires. In Forever a Bane and a Curse are separate, so you can have both.

Make Fear count

Fear (CC) + diminishing returns

Fear (1.5 sec cast, 20 yards) sends the enemy running; damage can break it, and with lots of effects on them it breaks sooner. Use it to gain distance or to cast something long in peace, and rely on effects you already applied instead of stacking more. Reapplied quickly on the same target, it lasts less each time (diminishing returns). Watch out for Undead (Will of the Forsaken) and warriors in Berserker Stance, who can break it.

Hide between DoTs

Line of sight (LoS)

Against hunters and casters, line of sight is your shield. Step out from behind a pillar, a house or a tree, apply an instant effect and duck back: your damage keeps ticking and they can't cast at you. They'll have to come closer, right where your Fear and your demon are waiting.

Death Coil at the right moment

Death Coil

Death Coil is instant: the enemy runs in horror for 3 sec, takes damage and you heal for that much. Use it when Fear breaks and they're back on you, to stop a cast you can't stop otherwise, or to survive one more hit. In Classic it doesn't share diminishing returns with Fear, so they chain well; that is not confirmed yet in Forever. It still works on a warrior in Berserker Stance.

Soul Link: your demon as a shield

Soul Link

With Soul Link (Demonology), your demon takes 30% of the damage aimed at you and you both deal 3% more. It only works while the demon lives: watch its health and use Health Funnel (Improved Health Funnel lets you use it at any health with no threat). Priests and shamans can dispel it off the demon. If it dies, Fel Domination and resummon it almost instantly.

Control your demon by hand

Pet control (Spell Lock / Seduction)

In PvP, don't leave your demon on autopilot. With the Felhunter, save Spell Lock for the enemy's key spell (a heal or a crowd control): landed mid-cast it also locks that school for 6 sec. Devour Magic strips a magic effect off you or a buff off the enemy. With the succubus or incubus, Seduction (humanoids only) locks the enemy down: don't put damage effects on them first or they'll break it.

Casting through pushback

Spell pushback

Every hit you take delays your cast-time spells. Forever's answers: the Voidwalker's Sacrifice gives you a shield, and while it holds you aren't pushed back; Fel Concentration (Affliction) gives Drain Life, Drain Soul and Wrack a 70% chance to ignore pushback. If a melee is on top of you, don't fight through a Fear cast: Death Coil or Howl of Terror (2 sec, up to 5 enemies within 10 yards) first.

In dungeons and raids

Let the Voidwalker tank

Pet tanking (threat)

Send the demon in first and wait until the mob locks onto it. With the Voidwalker, Torment pulls it back if it turns on you. Don't open with Searing Pain (high threat) unless you are Demonology and need it for Demonic Brand.

DoT and drain

DoT and drain

While leveling: Bane of Agony and Corruption, then Drain Life to heal while they tick (Soul Siphon drains more for each Affliction effect on the target). Bane of Agony hits hardest at the end: don't refresh it early. If the mob is almost dead, wand it instead of adding another effect.

Life as a resource

Life Tap cycle

Life Tap turns health into mana, and in Forever Spirit boosts it. Use it on the move or between effects, whenever you have more health than mana; then refill health with Drain Life. You'll barely need to stop and eat or drink.

Crowd control in groups

Crowd control (Fear / Banish)

In dungeons, Banish takes a demon or elemental out of the fight for up to 20 sec (it can't be damaged). Only Fear where there is room: a fleeing mob can run into another pack and bring it back. Curse of Recklessness stops it from fleeing, but also breaks your own Fear.

Multi-DoTting

Multi-DoT (DoT tab)

On packs or bosses with adds, Tab between them and put Corruption and Bane of Agony on each; only on mobs that will live long enough for the full tick. The more Corruption effects you have running, the more often Nightfall procs (instant Shadow Bolt).

Keybinds and macros

  • Move with W and S, strafe on A and D and turn with the right mouse button: it lets you apply effects while backing off.
  • Focus Fear macro: /cast [@focus] Fear. Set the second enemy as focus and fear it without losing your target.
  • Put your demon's skills on your own keys: a macro with /cast Spell Lock or /cast Seduction next to your spells, so you never click the pet bar.
  • Instants (Corruption, Bane of Agony, Curse of Exhaustion, Death Coil) on keys near WASD; Life Tap and Health Funnel further away.

Every technique, explained for any class: Gameplay techniques

Level 60

The beta only went up to level 30, so everything at 60 is theory built on the client's talents; nobody has tested it in raids. The most-planned level 60 build on the community calculator is Affliction with some Demonology for survivability. Raids open on 9 December; until then it is level 60 dungeons. We will update this part as soon as there is real data.

Affliction · Ranged DPS

Stats
  • Spell power (Shadow damage) first.
  • Spell hit up to the cap (Forever's cap is not confirmed yet).
  • Crit: in Forever damage over time can crit, and Malevolence and Pandemic multiply that.
  • Then Intellect, Stamina and Spirit (Spirit improves Life Tap). Haste does little for Affliction.
Rotation
  • Apply the Curse your group assigns you (Elements, Recklessness, Weakness or Tongues) and your Bane (Agony, or Doom if the fight is long: test it).
  • Keep Corruption and Siphon Life up at all times.
  • Wrack to keep its 10% boost on your Shadow damage over time.
  • Instant Shadow Bolt on Nightfall procs; in between, filler (Shadow Bolt or Drain Life).
  • Life Tap when mana runs low; Drain Soul at the end to restock shards.
Consumables
  • Spellstone on your weapon (a temporary enchant in Forever).
  • Spell power and Shadow damage elixirs, if they work as in Classic (to be confirmed).
  • Your own Healthstone and mana potions for long fights.

Destruction · Ranged DPS

Stats
  • Spell power (Fire and Shadow).
  • Crit: Ruin doubles the crit bonus of Destruction spells.
  • Hit up to the cap, then Intellect.
Rotation
  • Your assigned Curse and Immolate always up.
  • Conflagrate on cooldown: with Shadow and Flame 5/5 it does not eat Immolate and raises your Shadow damage by 10%.
  • Shadowburn when ready: with Shadow and Flame it refunds the shard and raises your Fire damage by 10%.
  • Incinerate as filler on a target with Immolate (25% more damage).
  • On packs of 4 or more: Bane of Havoc on one of the last enemies you will kill, then Hellfire or Rain of Fire.
Consumables
  • Firestone on your weapon if you mostly cast Fire; Spellstone if your filler is Shadow Bolt.
  • Spell power elixirs (still to confirm what exists in Forever).

Demonology · Pet DPS

Stats
  • Spell power: your demon scales with your stats and Demonic Knowledge adds up to 60 spell damage to both of you.
  • Stamina more than in other trees (Demonic Embrace multiplies it and it feeds Life Tap).
  • Hit and crit after that.
Rotation
  • With Demonic Pact: sacrifice the Imp (15% more Shadow damage), then summon the Succubus or Incubus (Master Demonologist adds another 10% Shadow).
  • Searing Pain to keep Demonic Brand up: your demon's next attacks deal bonus damage.
  • Corruption and Bane of Agony, with Shadow Bolt as filler.
  • With Decimation: below 35% health, Soul Fire is faster and costs no shard.
Consumables
  • Spellstone on your weapon.
  • Keep your demon alive: Health Funnel, or Fel Domination to summon another quickly.

Gear

There are no Forever gear lists for 60 yet: dungeon loot has been redone and raids open on 9 December. Meanwhile, look for cloth with spell power (or Shadow damage for Affliction and Fire for Destruction), then hit and crit, then Intellect and Stamina. Tailoring makes soulbound Shadow and Fire pieces that help a lot while leveling.

PvP at level 60

  • The Felhunter is the PvP demon: Spell Lock stops casts and Devour Magic cleanses you or strips enemy buffs.
  • Fear only reaches 20 yards in Forever: get just close enough and keep Howl of Terror or Death Coil ready if they reach you.
  • Use the split between Banes and Curses: Bane of Agony for damage plus Curse of Tongues on casters or Curse of Exhaustion on melee.
  • Demonology with Soul Link is the toughest option: your demon soaks part of the damage you take.
  • Against Orcs, watch out: Shatter Curse strips all your Curses and Banes at once. Against Undead, Will of the Forsaken breaks your Fear.
  • Succubus or Incubus Seduction sets up a damage window, but your damage over time breaks it, so do not apply it first.
  • In battlegrounds, a Soulstone on a healer and Healthstones for the whole group are worth as much as your damage.

Professions for this class

TailoringMakes soulbound Shadow and Fire pieces that are great for leveling, and its camp item gives Spirit, which improves Life Tap.
EnchantingPairs with Tailoring: disenchant what you make and craft wands, caster staves, off-hands and oils.
EngineeringFor PvP: gadgets and explosives that add control and extra escapes. It has new schematics in Forever.
First AidBandages to recover health between fights (and then Life Tap it into mana).
