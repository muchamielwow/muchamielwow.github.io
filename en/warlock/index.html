Which one to choose

Start by testing B1 against each class. Compare B2 if casting Corruption does not cost you too much. B6 and B9 are alternatives for a change of playstyle: drains or burst. B5, B8, B12 and B13 are niche options that need practice results to justify the tools they give up.

These preferences assess the available tools, rather than a simulation or tournament result. You can choose only one of these builds per phase. A legal point allocation does not prove that it is competitive.

Talent variants

  • Havoc against pet classes: in B9 change Agonizing Flames 2/3 to 1/3 and add Bane of Havoc 1/1. In B10 change Aftermath 4/5 to 3/5 and add Havoc. In B11 remove Molten Skin 1/5 and add Havoc. Totals remain at 26, with 20 points before its row. Use it on the enemy pet; it competes with other Banes on the same target. Against a single target without a pet, this point loses much of its value.
  • Shadow Mastery 1/5: in B6 you can remove 1 point from Malediction and put it here: 25 points remain before the row, with 26 total. You do not get 5/5. Compare overall Shadow damage against periodic damage.
  • Decimation: it does improve your execute attacks, but you cannot use the Soul Fire portion at level 30. I have not chosen it by default over pet replacement and survival.
  • Improved Felhunter: at level 30 you cannot use its Spell Lock cooldown reduction, since that spell is listed at level 36. Its Devour Magic improvement still matters; weigh which 1–3 points at that depth you would give up.
  • Soul Harvest: requires valid kills for its effect. I do not prioritize it in a dedicated dueling build.

Requirement sources: Forever calculator, Exhaustion and prerequisite, Soul Link and prerequisite, Master Summoner, Fel Domination, Demonic Knowledge, Master Demonologist, Ruin, Conflagrate, Havoc.

DU1 · Warrior
Link to DU1

Win condition

Survive reconnections while DoTs and your demon keep dealing damage. Win when they can no longer maintain contact and you have health left to keep pressure.

Enemy window: wait for Berserker Rage to end before relying on Fear or incapacitation. Death Wish also gives Fear immunity if talented. Respect Shield Wall and keep an answer to Intercept.

Build and talents: start with B1 for instant Corruption, Soul Link and demon replacement. If testing shows you cannot survive physical damage, compare the B1 flex option that changes Unholy Power 5/5 to 3/5 and Demonic Aegis 2/2. Only before locking in the phase. B7 provides Exhaustion and drains, but gives up Soul Link: compare it if your spacing and CC work, rather than treating it as an automatic upgrade.

Demon: Voidwalker to assess a shield; Succubus to test CC. The Voidwalker's Sacrifice consumes the demon and therefore your Soul Link support: do not press D4 out of habit or assume both defenses remain together.

Plan: respect their uncontested opener when applicable. Then pressure with instant spells, create distance and avoid standing still to cast under melee pressure. Use CC when it gives a real window to recover or reposition. If you see Fear immunity, do not spend your next actions repeating it: use damage, movement or defense and reassess CC when it ends. Watch Berserker Rage at this level according to the Forever Warrior spellbook.

Curse and buttons: C7 with Ctrl to test Weakness against physical pressure; A1 for Exhaustion only in B6/B7, remembering it changes your Curse. C1, C3, C8, C23, D1/D2 and D4 with a Voidwalker. R1 does not remove Hamstring or stuns.

Mistake to avoid: using too much Life Tap when your opponent is already close, or relying on a channel you cannot sustain. Note whether you lose because of your pet, lack of distance or having no defense left after Sacrifice.

DU2 · Rogue
Link to DU2

Win condition

Survive the first chain and apply pressure that makes resetting difficult. Do not face the second opener with no answer or your Shadow school locked by Kick.

Before finishing: identify Evasion, Vanish and their CC escape. Evasion limits attacks they can dodge; it is not magic immunity. A DoT can hinder a reset but can also break your own damage-sensitive CC.

Build and talents: B1 as your first test; use the same Demonic Aegis flex to compare physical defense. B9 is an alternative with Molten Skin and Intensity to seek a damage window after CC, but lacks Soul Link and talented demon replacement: compare survival before choosing it.

Demon: Voidwalker to test the shield, or Succubus to attempt CC when you can act. Pet abilities have their own conditions; do not promise a macro will work through every stun on its owner.

Plan: allow the tournament's stealth opener. Afterwards, stabilize your health before attempting a long damage chain. Watch Kick: protecting your Shadow school matters because several tools depend on it. You can cancel a cast with C18 to try to bait an interrupt, but your opponent can wait; do not turn the attempt into several seconds of doing nothing. The Forever Rogue spellbook lists Kick below level 30.

CC: decide whether to maintain Seduction before applying DoTs. For attrition, periodic effects can make it harder for your opponent to restealth, but do not guarantee stopping every defense or reset. Do not set up Seduction on a target covered in DoTs and expect a stable window.

Buttons: C1, C3/C24, C13 with a Succubus, C18, C23 and D4 with a Voidwalker. R1 works against valid Charm/Fear/Sleep CC, not Cheap Shot or Kidney Shot. Do not assume your trinket C19 removes every CC: it depends on the equipped item.

Mistake to avoid: using your stone, racial, trinket and shield in the same reaction without checking which effect you need to resolve. Record opener damage and the resources you keep afterwards.

DU3 · Mage
Link to DU3

Win condition

Maintain pressure that does not vanish with each Remove Lesser Curse and survive burst. Force Ice Block, save resources and resume damage after immunity.

Before finishing: identify Blink, Counterspell and Ice Block/Cold Snap if talented. Do not spend burst into immunity. Polymorph can let its target recover: a reset can help the opponent too.

Build and talents: B1 for damage while moving and survival; B2 if the extra power is worth it and you can cast Corruption. B6 is an attrition alternative with Nightfall and Siphon Life, but its drains and Fear remain exposed to interrupts.

Demon: Felhunter for Devour Magic. At level 30 you do not have Spell Lock: your demon does not provide an available interrupt in this guide. Separate your position from your demon enough to avoid both taking the same pressure, without losing useful range.

Plan: apply damage when authorized, protect your pet and choose when to use Devour on a dispellable magic effect on yourself or a dispellable enemy buff. Watch Counterspell; try casting when its use or your position creates a window. C18 lets you deliberately stop a cast, but does not force your opponent to spend their interrupt.

Forever-specific detail: Remove Lesser Curse can remove a Curse or Bane, according to the Forever Mage spellbook. Do not mechanically reapply every removed effect if it drains your mana. During Ice Block, do not waste finishers into immunity; reposition and prepare instead, without assuming an automatic purge interaction.

Buttons: C5/C6, C7 for Tongues, C12 to dispel/purge, C21 for your demon when applicable, C1, C3 and D1/D2. Will of the Forsaken does not remove Polymorph or Frost roots: R1 is not the answer to all their CC.

Mistake to avoid: letting Counterspell lock your Shadow school when you need Fear or Drain Life. Note how many Devours you used on the enemy and how many you needed for defense.

DU4 · Priest
Link to DU4

Win condition

Make their heals and dispels cost more than replacing your pressure. Against Discipline, win through resources and continuity; capitalize on a healing gap to finish.

Before finishing: distinguish Discipline/healing from Shadow. Dispel Magic, Psychic Scream and Mana Burn change priorities. Against healing seek an interrupt or CC window; against Shadow do not assume the same healing war.

Build and talents: B1 to sustain the duel with a demon; B6 to compare attrition and drain pressure; B9 if testing shows you can create a damage window their heals cannot recover from. Choosing B6 or B9 means losing Soul Link, rather than simply adding pressure to B1.

Demon: Felhunter. Devour can help against a dispellable magic buff or debuff; it does not automatically choose the shield or effect you want most when several are present.

Plan: maintain pressure without wasting resources immediately reapplying everything dispelled. Combine damage and CC so healing or dispelling costs them actions. Tongues can slow casts, but does not hinder instant abilities. Drain Mana is an option when you have a safe channel and a resource battle; do not use it if losing pressure or position hurts you more.

Undead: save R1 for Fear when breaking it lets you keep pressuring or survive. Do not use it preemptively: it no longer grants immunity. If your opponent is also Undead, they can answer your Fear with their racial; do not base your entire damage window on the first Fear lasting its full duration.

Watch for: Dispel Magic can remove magic debuffs from themselves or magic buffs from enemies, according to the Forever Priest spellbook. Check your active buffs and your demon's status during the fight instead of assuming everything remains ready from the opener.

Buttons: C7, C9, C12, C3, R1, C23 and D1/D2 with Soul Link; A2/A3 in builds that benefit from them, and T1/T2/T3 in B9. Common mistake: draining your own health with Life Tap to sustain a mana war you do not yet control.

DU5 · Hunter
Link to DU5

Win condition

Maintain damage while limiting free shots and controlling the pet when useful. Reacquire after Feign Death: your survival must outlast their pressure.

Before finishing: maintain range and reacquire after Feign Death, identify the trap and count the pet as another damage source. Controlling the owner does not automatically stop the pet.

Build and talents: B1 to withstand simultaneous pressure from the player and pet. B7 compares mobility, Exhaustion and drains with more health; B9 lets you experiment with burst and the Havoc variant already described, but that point and the loss of Soul Link need to be justified through practice.

Demon: Voidwalker if its shield helps sustain their damage, or Succubus to attempt CC on the Hunter. Do not Banish their pet just because it is a pet: C14 requires a demon or elemental, not any beast.

Plan: control the player or pet to reduce pressure while damaging your chosen target; use C4 to know which one your CC targets. If you Fear the pet, do not confuse its focus with the Hunter's on your next cast. Avoid chasing without watching where they force you to fight and where your demon is. Do not switch to hitting the pet on autopilot: decide whether spending damage on it gives you a real advantage.

Level 30: the Forever Hunter spellbook places Viper Sting at level 36, so do not build this duel around a mana drain outside this bracket. Also, do not assume pet abilities or talents you have not observed.

Buttons: C4, C3/C24, C1, C5/C6, C23 and D1/D2; D4 with a Voidwalker, C13 with a Succubus. A1 only with its talent. T5 only if you chose the legal Havoc variant; it does not come free in B9.

Mistake to avoid: spending so much CC and damage on their pet that the player is free to punish you. Note which target you controlled and how long your demon remained useful.

DU6 · Paladin
Link to DU6

Win condition

Maintain magic DoTs and attrition without running dry, and force Divine Protection before the finish. Purify does not remove magic at level 30; use that pressure without giving melee uptime.

Before finishing: watch Divine Protection, Blessing of Protection and Hammer of Justice. Physical protection does not stop all magic damage. At level 30 they lack Divine Shield and Cleanse; a healing build demands more pressure and resource management.

Build and talents: B1 for a first survival comparison; B6 for attrition with drains and Exhaustion; B9 if CC and concentrated damage work better against their heals. Choose between those complete toolkits without combining talents from all three builds.

Demon: Felhunter to attempt to purge dispellable buffs; Succubus to practice a CC window on a humanoid and direct damage. Seduction and several DoTs on the same target interfere with each other.

Plan: against melee pressure, assess your distance and do not overspend health with Life Tap. Against a healer playstyle, seek a CC window or wear them down without draining yourself first. Use Tongues for casts; do not expect it to affect instant heals. If B6/B7 uses Exhaustion, remember it replaces Tongues on that target.

Level matters: the Forever Paladin spellbook places Divine Protection before level 30, Divine Shield at 34 and Cleanse at 42. Do not copy a level 60 plan that gives them Divine Shield and Cleanse at 30. Protection from physical damage is also not immunity to your spells. Read the specific active effect and do not waste a finisher into an immunity that does block it.

Buttons: C7, C12, C3, C9 when draining is safe, C23; A1/A3 with Affliction, and T1/T2/T3 with B9. R1 does not remove Hammer of Justice; if the Paladin is Undead, account for their own racial answer to your CC.

Mistake to avoid: spending your entire offensive window during their protection, or wasting time trying to purge an undispellable effect. Note whether you lack pressure to finish or health to survive their return.

DU7 · Shaman
Link to DU7

Win condition

Open a Fear window after removing Tremor and keep Grounding from absorbing decisive control. Let DoTs work while they spend globals recovering.

Before finishing: Grounding can intercept a spell and Tremor counters Fear. Manually destroy the totem blocking your plan, not every totem by habit. Account for Earth Shock before an important heal or cast.

Build and talents: B1 to start; B2 to compare spell power if you can cast; B9 if you manage interrupt windows and seek concentrated damage. Against melee, test the defensive flex of B1 before locking in the phase; against a caster, do not assume more armor solves magic damage.

Demon: Felhunter for Devour and pressure on magic buffs; compare Voidwalker if physical pressure is killing you. Do not expect Spell Lock at 30.

Plan: watch totems before repeatedly using Fear or spending an important ability. Tremor removes Fear/Charm/Sleep and Grounding can redirect a spell; select and attack the relevant totem when warranted, then return to your target. C2 lets you order your demon to attack a mouseover enemy, but does not automatically identify or destroy a totem by name.

Interrupts: Earth Shock can lock your school; avoid offering a free Shadow channel when you need to preserve Fear. Tongues affects casts, rather than turning instant shocks into slow abilities. These tools appear in the Forever Shaman spellbook.

Buttons: C2/C1, C3/C24, C7, C12, C18, C23 and D1/D2. If they are an Orc using Shatter Curse, do not repeatedly reapply Curse/Bane during their racial immunity; use other tools and check the active effect. Its existence is confirmed by Blizzard: Forever racials.

Mistake to avoid: spending Fear into Tremor without dealing with it, or attempting a finisher while Grounding can intercept it. Record which totem was active when your CC failed.

DU8 · Druid
Link to DU8

Win condition

Deny full recovery and pressure when they leave form to heal. Maintain magic damage alongside effects they can remove; do not spend everything chasing Travel Form.

Before finishing: distinguish Feral from caster/healing. Shapeshifting can remove roots and slows but costs Mana. Bear Form handles physical damage better; healing in caster form opens an opportunity. At level 30 they have no Cyclone.

Build and talents: B1 to prepare for a phase with varied opponents. Against Feral pressure, compare the defensive B1 flex and Voidwalker; against healing/caster playstyles, compare Felhunter and B6/B7. Exhaustion and drains are tools to assess, rather than a guarantee of holding an opponent who shapeshifts.

Demon: Voidwalker against physical pressure, Felhunter for dispellable magic effects, or Succubus if you can use a window in humanoid form. Do not assume Seduction works in beast form: check their creature type and form before spending it.

Plan: if they open from stealth, respect their tournament opener. Maintain attrition without chasing in ways that surrender position or abandon your demon. Watch when they leave form to heal: you can attempt Tongues and CC in that window, subject to availability and range. Do not turn the fight into repeatedly reapplying the same Curse without watching their response.

Forever-specific detail: Remove Curse removes a Curse or Bane according to the Forever Druid spellbook. Maintaining damage means assessing what you can sustain against that cleanse, rather than assuming Agony stays because it is a Bane. Bleeds are not magic effects Devour cleanses by default, and R1 does not remove roots or stuns.

Buttons: C5/C6, C7, C3/C24, C1, C23; A1/A3 if you have those talents, D1/D2 with Soul Link, and D4 with a Voidwalker. R5 is recovery with a valid corpse, not part of a duel opener.

Mistake to avoid: attempting Seduction in an invalid form, or losing the fight by chasing a heal while running out of resources. Record which form and spec caused the problem.

DU9 · Warlock
Link to DU9

Win condition

Win the health, Mana and demon resource loop. Trade dispels and DoTs efficiently; switching to the pet is worthwhile only if it denies more survival than the free damage given to its owner.

Before finishing: identify the demon and Soul Link; not every build has it. At level 30 Felhunter has Devour Magic, but no Spell Lock (36); do not plan around Death Coil (42) either. Killing the pet is a pressure decision, not an automatic rule.

Build and talents: B1 as a first mirror test; B2 to compare power and resource management; B9 as a burst alternative if you can create the window. I do not recommend B12 by default: sacrificing your pet reduces precisely the tools that matter in this matchup.

Demon: Felhunter for Devour; watch both your pet's health and the enemy's position. Changing or recovering a demon has costs and conditions: D2 replaces it, and D1 is used after the summon finishes.

Plan: observe whether your opponent has Soul Link, which demon they use and whether they pressure your pet. Choose between maintaining damage on the player or spending actions controlling their demon; neither choice is automatically correct in every mirror. C14 can attempt Banish on a valid enemy demon, but need not be used on cooldown; do not assume untested Banish interactions with all their talents.

Devour and racial: C12 to cleanse yourself or purge a dispellable buff, C21 to attempt to cleanse your demon. They share Devour, so you do not have three independent dispels. Save R1 for Fear or Seduction when breaking it has value; if the opponent is Undead, they can also escape your CC. Neither gains subsequent immunity from Will of the Forsaken in Forever.

Do not copy higher-level abilities: Spell Lock, Shadow Ward, Death Coil and Howl of Terror are unavailable in this level 30 package. Check abilities and grimoires against the Forever Warlock spellbook.

Buttons: C4, C14, C12/C21, R1, C1, C11, C23 and D1/D2. T1 only with talented Shadowburn. Common mistake: using Life Tap until you enter finisher range, or letting your demon die while watching only the player's health.

Log to choose your phase build: play several repetitions per class under comparable conditions. Record your build, demon, opponent's racial, defensive item used and reason for losing: opener, interrupt, pet, mana or lack of damage. Seek a build covering the phase's most frequent problems, rather than changing points over a single loss.

BGs and open world

This guide is also designed for playing with other enemies and allies around. It keeps the level 30, 26-point budget: the following decisions are practice suggestions, rather than a proven ranking. If you level up or your budget changes, review talents, ranks and abilities; do not automatically add abilities excluded at level 30.

The Forever Open rules change often: always check the official rules. They do not apply to BGs or world PvP.

With multiple targets: set the enemy you want to CC as your focus with C4. Check your focus before pressing Shift. Mouseover can be a character or a valid frame; without one, check each macro's fallback. Do not spread damage on autopilot: help kill your group's chosen target and protect the teammate under pressure.

With teammates: C25 is dedicated to dispelling allies or yourself. C12 also allows purging enemies, and C21 attempts to cleanse your demon. All three spend the same pet ability: the macro does not create independent uses or cooldowns. Choosing who receives Devour is a gameplay decision, rather than automation.

CC and survival: C3/C24, C13 and C14 offer different recipients depending on what you want to control. Seduction requires special care around DoTs and pet damage. C18 stops your character's action, and C1 recalls your demon, but neither removes periodic damage already applied. C20 is for a bandage window, and C23 for the stone: incoming damage and item restrictions still apply.

Undead in world PvP: R1 breaks Charm, Fear or Sleep when needed; it does not protect against the next CC. R5 can help after a fight with a valid corpse and a safe position. Do not confuse Cannibalize with an instant heal: channeling means weighing the risk of another fight. Passives work without a button.

Between fights: prepare shards with C22 when appropriate, create your stone with C15, replace your demon and armor, and check your grimoires. Life Tap helps manage mana, but decide how much health you can spend before exposing yourself again. Drain Life and Drain Mana do not replace moving to safety.

First buttons to learn: C1/C2, C3 or C24, C4, C5/C6, C12 or C25 with a Felhunter, C20/C23 and R1. Add your build's tools afterwards. You do not need to learn 41 buttons at once: several blocks are alternatives for the same action or depend on talents and items you may not use.

Gear and BiS references

Check WOWF.IO level 30 BiS, selecting PvP, Horde and your specialization, and compare with The Forever Era BiS and Wowhead guides. The lists consulted disagree on several pieces, and some sources still use Classic data or higher-level quest rewards; a list alone does not confirm you can obtain every piece in this beta.

As a testing guideline: with Soul Link and Affliction, seek sufficient health, Shadow damage or general spell power, and mana; with Destruction, assess Fire damage/general spell power and crit without losing too much health. These are duel adaptation guidelines, rather than calculated stat weights. Shadow gear does not boost your Fire spells.

Items appearing in the lists to start checking availability: Filigreed Shadow Circlet, Shadow Gown, Hands of Darkness, Black Slippers. Level 30 guides also list Staff of the Shade (RFK), Blighted Leggings (Azshir), Bloodmage Mantle (Thalnos), Berylline Pads (RFK quest) and Warsong Sash (Horde quest). I do not present them as a universal BiS set for all thirteen builds. Check profession requirements: some Forever pieces are restricted to their crafter.

Gear and gameplay sources: Wowhead Demonology 30, Wowhead Affliction 30, Wowhead Destruction 30. These are PvE guides; I use them for ability and acquisition data, rather than certification of tournament performance.

What macros improve

Warlock examples; general guidance is in the macro guide.

Control without losing your target

Focus Fear and Seduction, or mouseover Devour Magic: keep your opponent targeted while controlling or dispelling where needed. Avoid selecting, casting and selecting again.

See Fear · C3

Protect your demon

Recalling it with one key and recovering it when appropriate helps keep it alive. With Soul Link, losing it affects your defense: managing it is part of your survival.

See pet recall · C1

React in time

The Fear macro stops your current cast to attempt CC when pressed. Deliberate access to shields and sacrifices saves searching for a button under pressure.

See shield · D4

Drain macros avoid restarting the channel when you press again. You can focus on positioning and watching the enemy.
