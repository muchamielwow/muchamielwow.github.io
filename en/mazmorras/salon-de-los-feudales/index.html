DUNGEON · LEVEL 13-18

Hall of Thanes

★ New in WoW Forever

Beneath Ironforge's throne room rest the ancient dwarven lords, and the Dark Hammer has invaded that sacred place to plunder it. The group makes its way through crypts with restless spirits, stone golems and Dark Iron dwarves to the Reliquary of Kings, where the disgraced thane awaits. It is a short dungeon, designed mainly for the Alliance, with the feel of an underground pantheon and forge fire.

How to get there

Ironforge: as you enter the High Seat, an open door immediately on the left leads to Old Ironforge; go all the way down to the bottom, where the portal is. No key or requirement. The Horde has to cross Hillsbrad, Arathi, the Wetlands and Loch Modan and get into Ironforge dodging the guards. Spirit healer right outside the city.

What changes in Forever

New WoW Forever dungeon: it didn't exist in Classic (Old Ironforge was closed off). Icy Veins, Wowhead and Warcraft Tavern agree on the 13-18 range. The beta loot requires level 13 to equip.

Tips

  • Clear the Enraged Apparitions in Anvilmar Rest before going for Faldrim: if he patrols in with them on top of you, the tank loses attack speed and threat.
  • On Magmatus, all damage on the Dark Iron Summoner first, and the tank drags the boss away from the group so the Fire Nova doesn't catch the casters.
  • In the Reliquary of Kings take your time: clear the edges, kill the golems before Durgen and open every chamber, since they hold the relics for «Important Heirlooms» and the treaty.
