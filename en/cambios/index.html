October 15 · Reworked racials, standing-still wands and new talents

OCTOBER 15

Reworked racials, standing-still wands and new talents

A big patch: almost every race's racials change, wands and Auto Shot require standing still for half a second, Mages get Frost Blast and Arcane Wrath, Shamans get Elemental Mastery back and Shadow Priests gain Dark Inspiration. Many auras become raid-wide.

This is a summary. Further down you have Blizzard's full patch notes, exactly as published.

Buffs

  • ▲New tutorial for new players and an “Auto Attack/Auto Shot” option so Hunters switch between them automatically (gamepad too).
  • ▲Battleground Marks of Honor stack up to 30 (was 20).

Changes and fixes

  • ●Wands and the Hunter's Auto Shot: you must stand still for 0.5 sec before shooting, as in early versions of WoW.
  • ●Many auras and shouts become raid-wide: Battle Shout, Trueshot Aura, Leader of the Pack, Moonkin Aura and the Grace of Air, Strength of Earth, Windfury and Flametongue totems.
  • ●Creatures regenerate health out of combat. Scrolls trigger the global cooldown. Draught of Predatory Senses becomes a combat potion with a shorter duration.
  • ●Dalaran: the Horde is no longer welcome in the city and is attacked on sight. The City of Dalaran dungeon is still accessible.
  • ●Legacy: Bartering no longer discounts training and repairs. The Quick and the Dead affects more Priest spells.
  • ●Interface: new unit frames, an equippable level column in the Auction House, more combat text options, and fewer nameplates for enemies far above or below you.

Races

Buffs

  • ▲Tauren: Endurance grants 2% critical damage and healing (was 1% hit). Plainsrunning also grants 3% mounted speed, but stutter-stepping no longer keeps it up. Cultivation down to 15 min (was 60) and War Stomp to 90 sec (was 2 min).
  • ▲Skyborne: Wind Blessed up to 3% haste (was 1%). Skysight and Read Leyline castable while moving. Walk on Air down to 90 sec (was 2 min). More Elemental Convergences and Leylines around the world.
  • ▲Night Elf: Quickness stacks with other movement speed increases.
  • ▲Troll: Regeneration up to 20% (was 10%).

Nerfs

  • ▼Orc: Axe Specialization becomes Savage (1% crit). Blood Fury up to 3 min (was 2). Shatter Curse no longer grants immunity: it dispels Curses and Banes and reduces magic damage taken by 5% (was 15%).
  • ▼Undead: no Will of the Forsaken under Scatter Shot. Touch of the Dead: 30% chance for casters and 20% for Paladins, Rogues and Warriors, with a 25 sec internal cooldown, and no longer scales with Shadow increases.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Dwarf: Mace Specialization becomes Earth Fury (2% critical damage and healing). Find Treasure can find extra items in chests.
  • ●Human: Sword Specialization becomes Ruthless (2% critical strike chance).
  • ●Gnome: Eureka! becomes an 8 sec buff with no charges and the same effect, now also for Power Word: Shield. Cannot be used in stealth or invisibility.

Druid

Buffs

  • ▲Faerie Fire generates threat in Bear Form. Furor keeps the Energy you had in Cat Form even if you shift to Bear.

Nerfs

  • ▼Omen of Clarity no longer procs from Faerie Fire in forms. Multiple Insect Swarms no longer stack the hit reduction.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Moonkin Aura grants only spell crit and Leader of the Pack only melee and ranged crit; both are raid-wide. Shred is learned at level 20 (was 22).
  • ●Fixed: high ranks of Rejuvenation no longer block other Druids' Rejuvenation, and some Feral talents now apply correctly on login.

Hunter

Buffs

  • ▲Fire traps scale with Attack Power and you can have one Fire and one Ice trap at once. Pets benefit from Expertise and inherit your higher crit chance.
  • ▲Beast Mastery: Summon Hawk spawns on and sticks to the target, and Improved Mend Pet now lowers Mend Pet's cost. Marksmanship: Trueshot Aura is raid-wide and Sniper Shot benefits from more talents.

Nerfs

  • ▼Auto Shot requires standing still for 0.5 sec before firing (it still fires while casting Aimed Shot or Sniper Shot).
  • ▼Pets: Dismember (Crocolisk) reduces healing by 25% for 6 sec every 15 sec (was 50%, 10 sec, every 6 sec). Sonic Blast (Bat) 30% for 20 sec every 60 sec (was 60%, 30 sec, every 30 sec). Mine! (Bird of Prey) 3 sec every 90 sec (was 4 sec every 60 sec).

Changes and fixes

  • ●Survival: Strider Kick usable while disarmed. Resourcefulness reduces its cost and procs on melee and ranged ability crits (not traps).

Mage

Buffs

  • ▲New spell Frost Blast (level 24): 1.5 sec cast, Frost damage and a 40% slow, using Frostbolt's talents. It replaces the Rank 1 Frostbolt trick.
  • ▲Arcane: new 16-point gold medal talent Arcane Wrath: Fireball-like damage with a 3.5 sec cast, plus damage based on 5% of your maximum Mana, 6 sec cooldown.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Arcane: Arcane Blast redesigned: 1.5 sec, and each cast grants 15% of your Intellect as spell damage for 15 sec, up to 4 times, with 40% less damage and coefficient. Missile Barrage moves to the first row as a 2-point talent.
  • ●Fire: Wake of Fire reverts to Improved Fire Blast (+8/15 yd range). Critical Mass goes up to 5 points (10%). Fire Power redesigned: 1/2/3% more Fire damage per Fire effect of yours on the target, up to 12%. Fire Blast grants an extra Heating Up charge.
  • ●Frost: Ice Lance vs frozen targets +200% (was +300%). Cone of Cold slows the first 4 targets more and for longer. Winter's Chill redesigned: your Frostbolt, Frost Blast and empowered Ice Lance can apply it, making your next Cone of Cold hit harder (up to 5 stacks).

Paladin

Buffs

  • ▲Righteous Fury: Holy damage threat at 90% (was 60%). Power Word: Shield no longer overwrites Seal of Fury.

Nerfs

  • ▼Consecration: 0.042 coefficient per tick (was 0.095). Fixed Seal of Righteousness getting almost double benefit from spell damage. Retribution: Champion of the Light grants 10/20/30% of Intellect (was 20/40/60%).
  • ▼Holy: fixed Infusion of Light giving Holy Shock 50% more damage against enemies.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Blessing of Salvation and its Greater version become Salvation and Mass Salvation, compatible with blessings. New Revoke to remove it from a group member.
  • ●Protection: Reckoning stores up to 4 charges again, released on your next swing as in Classic Era. Shield Specialization restores 4% Mana (was 6%).

Priest

Buffs

  • ▲Devouring Plague cooldown down to 24 sec (was 1 min). Vampiric Embrace to 30 sec (was 1 min) and it can no longer be resisted.
  • ▲Shadow: new talent Dark Inspiration (row 4): Mind Blast and a full Mind Flay give your party 3-15% Spirit for 10 sec. Improved Mind Flay: each tick hits 10/20% harder than the last. Devouring Contagion spreads to 2 targets. Improved Fade and Shadow Infinity removed.

Nerfs

  • ▼Shadow Word: Death cooldown up to 18 sec (was 12).

Changes and fixes

  • ●Discipline: Mental Strength moves to row 2 and grants 2-10% Intellect. Power in Light increases Smite and Penance if the target has Holy Fire or Shadow Word: Pain. Twin Disciplines now affects Shadow Word: Death, Mind Flay, Starshards and Penance.
  • ●Fixed: high ranks of Renew no longer block other Priests' Renew.

Rogue

Buffs

  • ▲Assassination: Cold Blood is correctly consumed when Mutilate crits.

Nerfs

  • ▼Poisons no longer proc against attackers. Sap no longer triggers weapon procs. You can no longer swap specs to refill Energy.

Shaman

Buffs

  • ▲Chain Lightning costs 35% less Mana. Grace of Air no longer conflicts with Windfury and Flametongue (those two still do), and all four totems are raid-wide.

Nerfs

  • ▼Fire Nova: much lower base damage, 0.143 coefficient (was 0.214) and much lower cost. Grounding Totem is destroyed after redirecting a spell.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Elemental: Elemental Mastery returns as the 31-point talent (−0.5 sec on Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst and no Chain Lightning cooldown for 15 sec, every 3 min). Lava Burst moves to row 5 as the 21-point talent, with a rank 4. Call of Thunder removed; Earthbound moves to row 4.
  • ●Enhancement: Stormstrike costs 8% of base Mana (was a flat 125) and its debuff is no longer consumed if the spell misses. Restoration: new Mana Tide Totem rank at level 38; Water Shield procs from any spell crit.

Warlock

Buffs

  • ▲Affliction: Shadow Mastery up to 2-10% Shadow damage (was 1-5%). Malediction becomes Grim Reach (+10/20% Affliction range), and Destructive Reach does the same for Destruction.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Fixed: you can cast Soul Link on your pet again, and the Incubus is no longer free to summon with the Reagent Economy Legacy perk.

Warrior

Buffs

  • ▲Battle Shout is raid-wide. Slam is no longer subject to spell pushback. Overpower works against much lower level enemies.

Changes and fixes

  • ●Fury: Lingering Rage no longer stops health regeneration after combat, and Improved Berserker Rage removes movement impairing effects. Arms: Sword Specialization no longer procs from Shield Bash or Shield Slam.
Blizzard's full patch notes

Bugfixes

Resolved an issue with extra bright fog at lower shadow settings.

Fixed an issue allowing Horde players to gain Kirin Tor reputation.

Creatures will now properly regenerate health when out of combat.

Fixed an issue with various Horde characters holding the Night Watchman's Torch.

Fixed an issue with various drink tooltips that were not displaying the correct amount of total Mana regeneration.

Changes and Updates

Added a new tutorial experience for new players.

Improved control over Lighting ‘Bloom Intensity’ in options to be a slider that has moved to the ‘Advanced’ graphics section.

Added ‘Auto Attack/Auto Shot’ setting. If enabled, Hunters will automatically switch between auto attack and auto shot. This is now also supported for gamepad.

Racial abilities have been updated.

Tauren

Endurance: Now grants 2% increased critical damage and healing (was 1% hit chance).

Plainsrunning: Also grants 3% increased mounted movement speed. This increase stacks with other mounted movement speed increases.

Plainsrunning now requires the Tauren to move a minimum distance per second to keep the effect.

Developers’ notes: Stutter-stepping is no longer sufficient to maintain the effect. This minimum does make some allowance for movement speed penalties.

Cultivation: Cooldown reduced to 15 minutes (was 60 minutes).

War Stomp: Cooldown reduced to 90 seconds (was 2 minutes).

Dwarf

Mace Specialization: Replaced with Earth Fury: 2% increased critical damage and healing.

Find Treasure: Now also grants a chance to find additional items in most chests.

Skyborne

Wind Blessed: Haste bonus increased to 3% (was 1%).

Skysight: Now castable while moving.

Read Leyline: Now castable while moving. Minimum duration increased to 30 seconds (was 20 seconds).

Walk on Air: Cooldown reduced to 90 seconds (was 2 minutes).

Increased the number of spawn locations of Elemental Convergences and Leylines in most Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor zones.

Human

Sword Specialization: Replaced with Ruthless: 2% increased critical strike chance.

Orc

Axe Specialization: Replaced with Savage: 1% increased critical strike chance.

Blood Fury: Cooldown increased to 3 minutes (was 2 minutes).

Shatter Curse: Now dispels Curses and Banes (was granting immunity). Magic damage taken reduced by 5% (was 15%).

Undead

Touch of the Grave: Proc chance and cooldown adjusted to make benefit more consistent. Improvements made to visuals.

Undead will no longer have Will of the Forsaken available to them while under the effects of Scatter Shot.

Touch of the Dead now has a 30% chance to activate for casters and a 20% chance to activate for Paladins/Rogues/Warriors, as well as a 25 second internal cooldown.

Touch of the Dead no longer scales with Shadow increases on the target or the caster.

Touch of the Dead no longer suffers from a decreased multiplier when a lower ranked spell is used.

Developers’ notes: This shouldn't have been in place because it's a racial, not a Class spell or ability.

Gnome

Eureka!: Now an 8 second buff with no charges. Same effect on damage and ability costs. Now also benefits Power Word: Shield.

Eureka cannot correctly be used while in stealth or invisibility.

Night Elf

Quickness: Movement speed increase now stacks with other movement speed increases.

Night Elves no longer display their shields while in Wisp Form.

Troll

Regeneration: Health regeneration bonuses increased to 20% (was 10%).

Classes

Wands now require you to stand still for 0.5 seconds before shooting, matching early versions of World of Warcraft.

Druid

Fixed an issue where high ranks of Rejuvenation were preventing other Druids from applying Rejuvenation to the target.

Omen of Clarity: Fixed a bug where this could be triggered by Faerie Fire while in Cat Form, Bear Form, or Dire Bear Form. Since Faerie Fire costs no resources in those forms, it cannot trigger Omen of Clarity.

Faerie Fire: Now generates threat while in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form (instead of just initiating combat at 0 threat).

Fixed an issue allowing multiple Faerie Fire debuffs to be active on a target.

Shred (Rank 1) is now learned at level 20 (was 22). It was possible to get Shredding Attacks before knowing Shred or Lacerate

Fixed an issue where Heart of the Wild, Feral Swiftness, Thick Hide, and Leader of the Pack were not applying correctly to characters when they logged in.

Balance

Multiple instances of Insect Swarm will no longer continue to reduce the target's hit chance.

Moonkin Aura now only increases Spell Critical Strike chance, and is Raid wide.

Feral

Leader of the Pack now only increases Melee and Ranged Critical Strike chance, and is Raid wide.

The flat damage on Shred's tooltip will now correctly scale with the % damage increase of the Savage Fury talent.

Developers’ notes: This was just a visual bug. The damage is unchanged.

Furor now allows the player to retain the Energy they had when they were last in Cat Form even if they go into Bear or Dire Bear Form.

Developers’ notes: Bear and Dire Bear Form still prevent Energy regeneration.

Hunter

Auto Shot now requires you to stand still for 0.5 seconds before shooting, matching early versions of World of Warcraft.

Developers’ notes: Your character will still fire Auto Shot while casting spells like Aimed Shot or Sniper Shot.

Arrows should now be visible when a character has their bow drawn back.

Pets now correctly benefit from Expertise.

The Hunter's Fire traps now correctly scale with Attack Power.

The Hunter may now correctly have 1 active Fire Trap and 1 active Ice Trap (was 1 Trap among all types).

Pets

Fixed an issue where pets were only ever inheriting the player's Melee Critical Strike chance instead of whichever is higher, melee or ranged.

Dismember (Crocolisk)

Healing reduction reduced to 25% (was 50%).

Duration reduced to 6 seconds (was 10 seconds).

Cooldown increased to 15 seconds (was 6 seconds).

Sonic Blast (Bat)

Cast speed reduction reduced to 30% (was 60%).

Duration reduced to 20 seconds (was 30 seconds).

Cooldown increased to 60 seconds (was 30 seconds).

Mine! (Bird of Prey)

Duration reduced to 3 seconds (was 4 seconds).

Cooldown increased to 90 seconds (was 60 seconds).

Beast Mastery

Summon Hawk will now correctly cause the Hawk to spawn at the target the spell was cast on.

Summon Hawk will now stick your Hawk to the target that it was summoned on.

Fixed an issue causing the Improved Mend Pet talent to increase the Mana cost of Mend Pet instead of decrease.

Marksmanship

Trueshot Aura is now Raid wide.

Fixed an issue where Sniper Shot was not benefitting from a few Marksmanship talents.

Survival

Strider Kick can now be used while disarmed or while no weapon is equipped in your Main Hand.

Resourcefulness now correctly reduces the Mana cost of Strider Kick.

Resourcefulness now correctly activates when landing a critical strike, but it has been changed to only activating on melee and ranged ability critical strikes to prevent spamming.

Developers’ notes: Is not activated by Traps.

Updated the tooltip for Predator's Edge changed to better convey that it only increases the critical strike damage of your abilities.

Mage

Frost Blast: This new spell is learned at level 24. It deals damage and slows the target's movement by 40%. It has a 1.5 second cast time, and is affected by all talents that affect Frostbolt.

Developers’ notes: The purpose of this spell is to provide the functionality formerly granted by Rank 1 Frostbolt despite the penalization of "down-ranking". It does have a higher Mana cost and deals a little more damage as well.

Arcane

Fixed an issue where Presence of Mind would not be consumed by spell casts.

Fixed an issue where Missile Barrage could reduce the cost and duration of a second Arcane Missiles cast.

Missile Barrage: This talent has moved to the first row and become a 2-point talent. It now also grants a chance for Arcane Missiles hits to trigger Missile Barrage.

Arcane Blast: Redesigned. This spell is now a 1.5 second cast, and each time it is cast it grants the casting Mage 15% of their Intellect as additional Spell Damage. This effect also increases the cost of Arcane Blast as before and still stacks to 4 times. However, the effect now lasts 15 seconds from when it is first applied, instead of just until the next spell cast. Mana cost unchanged, but damage done and Spell Damage coefficients both reduced 40%.

Arcane Wrath: New 16-point gold medal talent. This new spell deals base damage similar to Fireball, with a 3.5 second cast, but also deals damage as if the Mage had additional Spell Power equal to 5% of the Mage's maximum Mana. 6 second cooldown.

Fire

Heating Up is no longer consumed if the Mage also had Presence of Mind active for the Pyroblast cast. Heating up also always gains an additional charge each time the Mage casts Fire Blast. This does mean Fire Blast critical strikes generate 2 charges.

Wake of Fire: Talent reverted back to Improved Fire Blast and no longer grants a bonus when the Mage kills a target. It also now increases the range of Fireblast by 8/15 yards.

Critical Mass: Mages can now spend up to 5 points in this talent for 10% total critical strike increase.

Fire Power: Redesigned. Now only 3 talent points. Increases damage done by Fire spells by 1/2/3% per the Mage's Fire effects on the target, up to a maximum increase of 12%. Spells that mix multiple schools of magic do not grant this bonus.

Developers’ notes: Specifically, the effects which can grant increased Fire damage from this talent are Ignite, Improved Scorch, Fireball, and Pyroblast, and Impact. Frostfire Bolt does not grant the damage increase but can benefit from it.

Frost Channeling now correctly reduces the Mage's threat.

Fixed an issue where Ice Block was too transparent.

Fixed various issues around Clearcasting. Clearingcast now correctly does not roll for every target struck by a spell. Clearcasting no longer has a chance to activate off of Dampen Magic and Amplify Magic. Clearcasting no longer has a chance to be activated by abilities cast when not in combat.

Frost

Fixed an issue preventing Frostbite and Fingers of Frost from activating from your Ice Armor and Frost Armor spells.

Ice Lance damage against Frozen targets changed to a 200% increase (Was 300%).

Cone of Cold: Movement speed of the first 4 targets hit is decreased by an additional 10% and the slow lasts an additional 3 seconds on those targets.

Developers’ notes: The ability of this spell to control massive number of targets was the primary reason it was previously reduced in effectiveness and duration. It feels reasonable to let it retain this level of power when used against 4 or fewer targets.

Winter's Chill: Redesigned. No longer increases critical strike chance. Instead, it causes Cone of Cold to deal 40% increase damage per stack of Winter's Chill on the target, consumed by next Cone of Cold.

Slightly changed the positioning of the spell visual for Cone of Cold.

New spell Frost Blast. 1.5 second cast. Deals low frost damage. Slows/chills. Benefits from talents that benefit Frostbolt.

Winter's Chill change to: Gives your Frostbolt, Frost Blast, and Fingers of Frost empowered Ice Lance spells a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to apply Winter's Chill, increasing the target's damage taken from your next Cone of Cold by 5% and it gains an additional 7% of your spell damage, stacking up to 5 times.

What changes from WoW Classic to WoW Forever

If you're coming from Classic, this is what you'll find different. Everything comes from what Blizzard has published; what it hasn't confirmed yet (dual spec, bank, WoW Token, layers…) is left out.

World and realms

What it is

Forever Blizzard calls it a third version of the game, alongside retail and Classic: it's not a mode, a season or another Classic. The story is set after Warcraft III and before Molten Core.

What's next for WoW Forever

Realms

Classic You pick a specific realm.

Forever No realms: you pick a ruleset (Normal, with PvP only if you opt in; PvP; or Roleplay). Hardcore arrives after launch. You only group with players on your ruleset, and Horde and Alliance still can't group together.

Deep dive (BlizzCon panel) · What's next for WoW Forever

Names

Classic Unique name on your realm.

Forever First name and second name (like a surname). Only the full combination must be unique across the region; a guild can share a surname and you can hide it on nameplates.

Name reservation

More world

Classic The 2004 map.

Forever Over 1,000 new quests from 1 to 60, new zones (Zephras Isle, Riverglades, Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, Darkwhisper Gorge) and visual touch-ups to familiar zones. No flying and no level scaling.

Forever The Skyborne, on both factions, with their own druid forms. They come with the Heroic pack and above.

Trees

Classic 1-point milestones at 11, 21 and 31.

Forever Same structure and rows, with a new milestone at 16. Some talents become baseline abilities (Blessing of Kings, Divine Spirit, Improved Mark of the Wild).

Classic Separate for melee, ranged and spells.

Forever Unified: helps hybrids, taunts, poisons and traps.
