WOW FOREVER · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Updated October 30, 2026

The questions that come up most in chat, with a short answer and the link where you'll find everything. In Muchamiel's chat, !faq brings you here.

LaunchPrice and packsCharacter and serversLevelingGold, professions and gearPlaying betterThe community

Launch

When does WoW Forever come out?

Thursday November 5 at 00:00 Spanish time (Wednesday November 4, 15:00 PST). In Mexico it's Wednesday the 4th at 17:00, and in Chile and Argentina the 4th at 20:00. More →

Do you start at level 60?

No: everyone starts at level 1. The level cap is 60 from day one. More →

Does anything carry over from the beta?

No. Whatever you leveled in the beta doesn't carry over: everyone starts from scratch. More →

When do raids open?

On December 9: Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia's Lair (40). More →

Will it run on my graphics card?

Blizzard's minimum is an AMD GCN 1 (2012), NVIDIA Maxwell (2014) or Intel Skylake (2015) graphics card, or newer. To check yours on Windows: Settings › System › Display › Advanced display. More →

Price and packs

How much does it cost to play?

You only need a subscription or game time: from €12.99 a month (€10.99 a month paying 6 or 12 months). The packs are optional. More →

Do I need to buy a pack?

No. The Heroic pack (€29.99) is only needed to play the new race (Skyborne) and to reserve names; the Epic pack adds 30 days of game time. More →

How do I pay less?

The 6 or 12 month subscription is the cheapest in the long run; to try it without commitment, a 60-day card. More →

How does Recruit-a-Friend work?

The Heroic pack comes with 1 invite code; the Epic pack and the Collection, 3. They arrive by email from October 20. Your friend plays free from November 4 to 11 (Pacific time). The codes can't be used to gift the game. More →

Character and servers

Which class should I play?

It depends on what you enjoy: we compare all nine (difficulty, leveling, groups and PvP) so you can pick in two minutes. More →

Which race should I pick?

Each class guide lists its recommended races, and the races page has every Forever racial. More →

How do names work?

There are no realms: each character has a first and last name, and the combination must be unique across the region. Names count across all rulesets. More →

PvP, Normal or RP?

On PvP rules you can be attacked in the open world; on Normal, only if you choose to. There are also RP and RP-PvP rules. More →

Can I hide my last name?

Yes: Options › Nameplates › My Secondary Name. It hides it above your character, but it still shows in chat. Several characters can share the same last name. More →

Can I turn transmog off?

Yes. At the transmogrifier you can switch it off completely and see everyone in their real gear. Classic Mode turns it off for you, but you keep collecting appearances. Collections stay hidden until you find what is in them.

Leveling

How do I level faster?

Dungeon quests, Well Fed food, the Cozy Sleeping Bag, logging out in an inn and the Well Rested Legacy perk. All five are explained. More →

Is there an addon that tells me which quest to do?

Yes: Questie marks quests on the map, and RestedXP or Polestar guide you step by step. We checked which ones already work in Forever. More →

Which talents should I take?

Each class guide has the point-by-point talent order from 10 to 60, checked against the Forever tree. More →

When do I get a mount and how much is it?

At level 40: 100 gold for riding and about 20 for the mount (at 60, 1,000 and about 200; it is not yet clear whether the first mount is included). Honored with your faction takes 10% off. Warlocks and paladins get theirs with a quest. More →

What do I lose if I resurrect at the spirit healer?

In the beta, 25% durability on all your gear (it was 50% in Classic). Resurrection Sickness works the same as always. If your corpse is close, running back to it is still cheaper.

Gold, professions and gear

Which professions should I pick?

While leveling, two gathering ones (they pay for your mount); later, any crafting one you like. There's a 1 to 300 guide for all twelve. More →

How do I make gold?

Gathering and selling on the auction house is the safest. Note: Forever has nerfed Classic tricks, like vendoring wands. More →

Are there BiS gear lists?

Not reliable ones yet: Blizzard hides loot until someone gets it. We list the trinkets and key items that are known. More →

What is the Legacy system?

Account-wide perks you earn with achievements and share across all your characters: faster leveling, cheaper flights, more honor… More →

What rewards does Legacy give?

On top of the perks, there are prizes for the points your account earns, spent or not: 15 gets the Replica Ironforge Air Rifle toy, 25 the Spectral Bear Cub pet, 40 the Spectral Bear Tabard and 55 the epic mount Reins of the Spectral Bear. You collect them from Wiley the innkeeper in Ratchet. Without the 12 PvP challenges you top out at 53, so the mount needs some PvP. Seen in the beta and in guides: Blizzard hasn't published the list. More →

Can I respec my Legacy points?

Yes. Points are earned account-wide, but each character spends them its own way, and they can be reset. In the beta a reset cost 10 gold (not confirmed for launch). A plan that works: while leveling, put everything into Professions to make gold and level your crafts; at 60, reset and switch to what you'll actually do (more honor or more reputation). Note that Dedicated Study (+1 to your lowest profession) could only be used once every 24 hours in the beta. More →

Playing better

Which addons do I need?

The basics that work in Forever (Questie, Auctionator, bags…) plus the PvP and Forever addons we recommend. More →

What are kiting, strafing and jump-turns?

Techniques expert players use to move and fight at the same time. We explain them all, with keybinds. More →

What do I do at level 60?

Fast riding, level 60 dungeons, reputations and getting ready for the December 9 raids. It's all in order. More →

Which websites help while playing?

For a second screen: Travelcraft (wow-travelcraft.com), a map with Forever's boat, zeppelin and flight routes that works out the fastest path and how long it takes; ForeverChanges (foreverchanges.pro), talent and spell changes versus Classic, a talent calculator, Legacy, professions, dungeons and items; Wowhead (wowhead.com/forever), news and database; WoW-Professions (wow-professions.com), long-standing profession guides with a Forever section; and Everloot's Raid Buff Planner (everloot.app/tools/raid), to sort buffs for 10, 20 and 40-player raids. Damage sims (such as MythicSim) are still in beta for Forever: don't pick a class based on them.

The community

Where's the Discord?

In chat, !discord. That's where groups, the guild and stream alerts are organized. More →

What do subs get?

The guides stay free for everyone. Subs get their own area: new stuff first, voting on the next guide, giveaways and events. You log in with Discord. More →
