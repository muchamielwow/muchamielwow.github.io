WOW FOREVER · CLASS GUIDE

Rogue

Updated October 30, 2026

OverviewLeveling 1-60How to playLevel 60PvPProfessionsStill to comeBeta (level 30)Calculator

Overview

The rogue is the melee specialist who strikes from the shadows: stealth, poisons, stuns and heavy single-target damage. Forever adds Mutilate and Venom (Assassination), lets rogues use axes, and a single talent, Hack and Slash, now works with every weapon type. It suits players who like choosing when a fight starts and who ends it.

Strengths

  • High single-target damage and strong finishers.
  • You choose when the fight starts: stealth, Sap and openers from behind.
  • Plenty of escapes: Evasion, Sprint, Vanish, Gouge and Blind.
  • In PvP, near-constant control with Cheap Shot, Kidney Shot and Kick.
  • Energy comes back smoothly (10 per second, no tick to wait for), so the pace feels smoother than in Classic.

Weaknesses

  • No self-healing: you rely on bandages, food and potions.
  • Almost no area damage: only Blade Flurry (Combat) reaches a second target.
  • Brings little raid utility beyond damage and Expose Armor.
  • If several enemies catch you with your defences on cooldown, you go down fast.

The specs

CombatMelee DPS · Leveling, dungeons and raids
The simplest and most consistent tree. Hack and Slash makes every weapon type viable, Riposte and Deflection keep you alive, and Blade Flurry adds some two-target damage. It is the most planned level 60 build in the calculator.
AssassinationMelee DPS (poisons) · Raids and PvP with daggers
The reworked tree: Mutilate (two daggers, 2 combo points) and Venom, which boosts your poison damage and how often they apply. It feels thin until level 30, then grows fast. Needs daggers in both hands.
SubtletyMelee DPS (stealth and bleeds) · PvP and duels
Better stealth, Premeditation, Preparation and cheap tricks (Sap and Blind cost half with Dirty Tricks). It has two new paths: bleeds with Hemorrhage, Rupture and Thousand Cuts, or Ambush with Cutthroat. It is the most planned tree at 60, mostly for PvP.

Recommended races

Human (Alliance)Ruthless gives a permanent +1% crit and Will to Survive breaks stuns: good for both PvE and PvP.
Night Elf (Alliance)Elune's Light: +10% crit for 15 sec. Quickness makes you harder to spot in stealth and Shadowmeld is one more escape.
Gnome (Alliance)Expansive Mind gives 5% more maximum Energy, Eureka! makes your strikes cheaper and stronger, and Escape Artist breaks slows.
Dwarf (Alliance)For PvP: Stoneform clears poisons, bleeds and diseases and cuts physical damage. +1% hit from Artisan's Precision.
Orc (Horde)Blood Fury: +10% Attack Power for 15 sec. Hardiness shortens stuns on you by 20%. The most complete Horde pick.
Undead (Horde)The PvP pick: Will of the Forsaken breaks fears and sleeps, and Touch of the Grave drains health as you hit.
Troll (Horde)Berserking: +10% attack speed for 15 sec, plus health regeneration for fewer breaks while leveling.
Skyborne (Windshaper) (Horde)New race (Heroic pack). Wind Blessed gives a flat +2% Haste and Skysight adds movement speed. The Alliance has its own version (High Order).

Leveling 1-60

Leveling spec: Combat

For leveling we recommend Combat: almost every guide agrees, and it is the tree that depends least on gear. You build with Sinister Strike, spend combo points on Slice and Dice and Eviscerate, and survive with Riposte, Evasion and Sprint (which Endurance brings back much sooner). Thanks to Hack and Slash any weapon works: swords and axes give extra swings, daggers and fist weapons give crit, maces ignore armor. Put your slowest, hardest-hitting weapon in the main hand. The Assassination points come at the end to complete the most played level 60 build.

How to kill while leveling

  1. Open from stealth: Cheap Shot (from 26) or Garrote; with a dagger, Ambush. On weak enemies, just walk in without stealth.
  2. Build points with Sinister Strike. Never use a builder at 5 points.
  3. High-health enemy: Slice and Dice at 1-2 points, then Eviscerate at 4-5.
  4. Normal enemy: Eviscerate at 3-5 points; skip Slice and Dice if it will die first.
  5. Use Riposte whenever it lights up after a parry.
  6. Two or more enemies: Blade Flurry and Evasion; Adrenaline Rush to end the fight fast.
  7. Kick casters and Gouge to buy time. If it all goes wrong, Vanish or Sprint.

Poisons and lockpicking

  • When: poisons arrive at level 20 through a class quest (guides place it between levels 20 and 24). At that point you learn Instant Poison and Crippling Poison; later come Mind-numbing (24), Deadly (30) and Wound (32), with new ranks up to 60.
  • For leveling: Instant Poison on both weapons. Fights are short, so instant damage pays off more.
  • At high level and in raids: Instant Poison on the slow weapon (usually the main hand) and Deadly Poison on the fast one (the off-hand). Assassination also keeps Venom up.
  • In PvP: Crippling Poison against runners and kiters, Wound Poison against healing and Mind-numbing against casters.
  • New in Forever: poisons can crit, scale with Attack Power, have more charges and stack with sharpening stones and weightstones on the same weapon. Malice and Precision now count for them.
  • Lockpicking: Pick Lock is learned from the trainer at level 16 and needs Thieves' Tools. It opens locked chests, the Junkboxes you get from Pick Pocket and some dungeon doors that work as shortcuts.
  • How to raise it: the skill goes up by opening locks at your level. Open the Junkboxes you steal as you level and you will not fall behind. How high it can go depends on your character level.

Tips

  • Level First Aid and Cooking from the start: in Forever First Aid makes healing potions and food gives bonus experience.
  • Sap one humanoid in a group and fight the rest: in Forever Sap no longer breaks stealth.
  • Visit your trainer every two levels: rogues get new ranks very often.
  • If you find a good sword, mace or fist weapon, train that skill in a capital city. One-handed axes are now allowed too.
  • With the Talented Legacy perk your first talent arrives at level 5, moving the whole plan 5 levels earlier.
  • Skinning gives you money and leather without detours: you are already killing beasts.
  • In dungeons, attack from behind and use Feint if the enemy turns on you.
  • Weapons you can learn: daggers, fist weapons, one-handed maces and swords and, new in Forever, one-handed axes; at range, bows, crossbows, guns and thrown. Your race already knows some; the rest come from a weapon master. Each skill costs 10 silver from a capital-city weapon master and can be learned from level 1; polearms cost 1 gold and need level 20. Learn one-handed swords, maces and axes early so you can put any slow drop in your main hand, and daggers if you plan to play around Backstab or Ambush. A ranged weapon lets you pull enemies one at a time.

Level faster with the 5 experience boosts (food, sleeping bag, tent, inn and Legacy).

How to play

In PvP

Open and chain stuns

Opener / stunlock

From stealth, Cheap Shot stuns for 4 sec and gives 2 combo points. Build points with your generator and, right as the stun is about to end, not before, cast Kidney Shot (up to 6 sec with 5 points at rank 2). Casting it while the first stun is still running wastes control time. As Subtlety, Premeditation gives you 2 points before the opener (spend them within 20 sec) for a longer chain.

Don't repeat the same kind of control

Diminishing returns (DR)

Against players, each control effect of the same type you repeat in a short time lasts half as long, then a quarter, and then the target is immune until about 15-20 sec pass without it (that is how Classic works; Forever keeps it and has announced no changes). So after your stuns, switch type: Gouge is an incapacitate and Blind is a different family, so they still last their full duration. If someone else already stunned your target (say with Hammer of Justice), your Kidney Shot may come out shortened: wait or use another control.

Gouge and get behind

Gouge and reposition

Gouge incapacitates a target facing you for 4 sec (longer with Improved Gouge), and any damage breaks it. Use it to stop a spell when Kick is on cooldown, or to buy time: after Gouge, stop attacking, pool energy and walk to their back. When they wake up you're not in front of them, and you can follow with Backstab or your next Kidney Shot. Watch your poisons and bleeds: if they're still ticking, they break Gouge.

Blind to bandage or restealth

Blind + bandage / restealth

Blind disorients for up to 10 sec at 10 yards and needs Blinding Powder. Any damage breaks it, so don't cast it with Garrote, Rupture or damage poisons still running. After Blind, step away and use a bandage (First Aid) or go back into Stealth to open again with Cheap Shot. Against a target that heals or still has its escape ready, hold it until they spend it.

Kick the spell that matters

Interrupt timing / juke

Kick stops the cast and locks that school for 5 sec (10 sec cooldown; Improved Kick also silences for 2 sec). Don't spend it on the first cast: good casters start one and cancel it (juke) to make you miss. Wait until the bar is past halfway on the heal, fear or Polymorph. Don't leave it to the very last moment: Forever has spell batching and the spell may still land. If Kick is on cooldown, Gouge interrupts too.

Vanish to reset the fight

Vanish reset

Vanish (Flash Powder, 5 min cooldown) puts you back in stealth and clears slows. It's not just for running: if the fight turns, Vanish, step away a couple of yards, let energy refill and open again with Cheap Shot. As Subtlety, Preparation (10 min) finishes the cooldown on Vanish, Blind, Sprint and Evasion: it's a whole second fight.

Sap and Distract to pick your fight

Sap and pick your fights

In Forever, Sap doesn't break your stealth: if two enemies travel together, Sap one (up to 45 sec, out-of-combat humanoids only) and open on the other. Distract turns enemies around and also lowers their stealth detection for 10 sec: use it to slip past a guard or behind a player unseen. With Sap, note that using it on a PvP-flagged target flags you too.

Don't let them get away

Sticking / slows

Run Crippling Poison on one weapon against runners: 70% slower for 12 sec. If you get slowed, Sprint with Improved Sprint removes the slow when you press it. Against mages and hunters, stick to their back: give them room and they'll slow you and get away. Against melee, Evasion (and Ghostly Strike as Subtlety) lets you win the trade.

In dungeons and raids

Always from behind

Positioning

From behind the enemy can't parry or block your attacks, and Backstab, Ambush and Garrote require it. In groups, stand behind the target, never between it and the tank. Solo leveling, if a hard hitter is facing you, Gouge and get behind it.

Pool energy before spending

Energy pooling

Energy refills at the same rate whether full or not, so sitting at max wastes it. Before a key moment (refreshing Slice and Dice, opening from stealth or popping Adrenaline Rush) let it build, but don't sit at 100. At 5 combo points don't use a generator: spend with a finisher first.

Manage your threat

Threat / Feint

A well-geared rogue easily pulls aggro off the tank early in a fight. Let the tank hit for a few seconds before you start and use Feint (10 sec cooldown) on cooldown on bosses. If the enemy turns to you, Evasion buys time while the tank takes it back; if someone is about to die, Vanish drops your threat.

Sap before the pull

Crowd control (Sap pull)

In dungeons, a Sapped humanoid is out for 45 sec and, in Forever, you stay in stealth. Agree with your group: you Sap the marked target and the tank pulls the rest. If it wakes early, Gouge or Blind buys a few seconds.

Kick rotation in groups

Interrupt rotation

Plenty of dungeon enemies heal or cast dangerous spells. Agree with the warrior or another rogue who interrupts what, so you don't burn two Kicks on one cast. Always kick heals and fears; small damage spells only when spare.

Keybinds and macros

  • Turn with the right mouse button and use A/D to strafe: it's how you circle a target to reach its back without wasting time.
  • Put Kick, Gouge, Kidney Shot, Blind and Vanish on keys you can hit without leaving WASD (1-5, Q, E, R, F or Shift+number). Don't put them where you have to look at your bars.
  • Focus macro to control a second enemy without switching targets: /cast [@focus] Blind. Forever supports focus macros.
  • Put Sprint, Evasion and your bandage on Shift+key: they are emergency buttons, and this way you won't waste them by accident.

Every technique, explained for any class: Gameplay techniques

Level 60

The level 60 builds come from the calculator using beta client data, and the beta stopped at level 30: nobody has played 60 in Forever yet. Guides give general priorities and are waiting for the first logs to fine-tune stats and gear. Treat this as a starting point.

Combat · Melee DPS · 18/33/0

Stats
  • Agility first: it gives Attack Power, crit and dodge.
  • Hit until you stop missing, then crit and Attack Power; the exact cap in Forever is not confirmed yet.
  • Strength only gives Attack Power: below Agility.
Rotation
  • Open with Cheap Shot or Garrote (Ambush with a dagger).
  • Keep Slice and Dice up at all times; refresh it before it drops.
  • Expose Armor at 5 points on bosses if nobody applies a better armor reduction.
  • Sinister Strike to build; Eviscerate with spare points.
  • Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush together at the start or when there are two targets.
Consumables
  • Instant Poison on the main hand (slow) and Deadly Poison on the off-hand (fast).
  • Sharpening stones or weightstones: in Forever they stack with poisons.
  • Thistle Tea for Energy, Agility food and elixirs, and healing potions.

Assassination · Melee DPS · 38/11/2

Stats
  • Agility and Attack Power: poisons now scale with it.
  • Crit: it triggers Seal Fate and poisons can crit.
  • Hit so neither strikes nor poisons miss (Precision now affects them).
Rotation
  • Open with Ambush from stealth.
  • Mutilate as your builder; at 4 points, Sinister Strike so you don't overcap.
  • Keep Venom, Slice and Dice and, on bosses, Expose Armor up; refresh whichever falls first (usually Venom).
  • Eviscerate only if it doesn't delay Venom or Slice and Dice.
  • Cold Blood on Mutilate or Eviscerate for a guaranteed crit.
Consumables
  • Two daggers: Mutilate requires them.
  • Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off-hand.
  • Thistle Tea, Agility food and elixirs.

Subtlety · Melee DPS / PvP · 16/12/23

Stats
  • Agility, Stamina and Attack Power: in PvP surviving matters as much as hitting.
  • Crit for your Ambush openers.
Rotation
  • Premeditation, then open with Cheap Shot or Ambush.
  • Kidney Shot at 5 points as the first stun ends.
  • Gouge, Blind or Vanish to reset; Preparation to get it all back.
  • Finish with Eviscerate.
Consumables
  • Crippling or Wound Poison depending on the opponent, plus Instant Poison.
  • Plenty of Blinding Powder and Flash Powder.
  • Healing potions and Thistle Tea.

Gear

There is no Forever best-in-slot list yet: dungeons have reworked loot and raids arrive on 9 December. Look for leather with Agility, hit and crit. Combat can use any weapon (swords and axes are the safest bet); Assassination needs two daggers; Subtlety wants a main-hand dagger for Ambush and Backstab.

PvP at level 60

  • Subtlety is the PvP tree: Premeditation, Preparation and Dirty Deeds give you long openers and a second attempt.
  • Chain Cheap Shot into Kidney Shot, but keep Gouge, Blind or Vanish for when the opponent breaks free.
  • Against casters, Kick the spell that matters (a heal or crowd control), not the first one you see. Combat can silence for 2 sec with Improved Kick.
  • Pick poisons per opponent: Wound against healers, Crippling against runners and Mind-numbing against casters.
  • Sap doesn't break stealth and Distract lowers the enemy's stealth detection: use them to choose who you fight.
  • In battlegrounds, hunt lone casters and healers; avoid long fights against several enemies.
  • Engineering adds grenades and gadgets that win duels; a PvP rogue almost always has it.

How to win this duel

Win condition = the situation you need to create to finish the duel: resources, range, control and a spent defensive answer. Tactical proposals by Van for level 30 with 26 points (21 + 5 Legacy); use only abilities and talents you actually have. Adapt to enemy spec, race and gear. These are not measured outcomes or guaranteed wins. Respect the current rules on boundaries and consumables.

Abilities by level: Talents Forever · Beta reference checked on Oct 9, 2026.

First build to test in each matchup. Identify your opponent's spec, race and gear: class alone isn't enough. Practice several rounds before changing points; the suggestions below are tactical proposals, not measured results.

Against Warrior
Direct link

Win condition

Gain an advantage in the opener and reset before an unfavorable sustained trade. Reopen with resources when defensives no longer cover your chain.

Enemy window: wait for Berserker Rage to end before relying on Fear or incapacitation. Death Wish also gives Fear immunity if talented. Respect Shield Wall and keep an answer to Intercept.

First test: Subtlety / Preparation · 4/0/22.

Their threat is keeping you in melee. Watch Charge/Intercept, Hamstring and shield swaps; an available Berserker Rage can shut down a fear. A brief gap doesn't mean a safe reset.

Armor and physical counters reduce the effectiveness of trading head-on. Subtlety lets you compare CC and resets; save defenses for their pressure.

Against Paladin
Direct link

Win condition

Force protections without spending all your damage and regain control after they end. Kick on a decisive heal should turn the second opener into a kill.

Before finishing: watch Divine Protection, Blessing of Protection and Hammer of Justice. Physical protection does not stop all magic damage. At level 30 they lack Divine Shield and Cleanse; a healing build demands more pressure and resource management.
